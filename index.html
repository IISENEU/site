About

Who we are

We're Northeastern's student chapter of the Institute of Industrial & Systems Engineers. Industrial engineering is about making complex systems work better, from hospitals and supply chains to factory floors and software. Through workshops, company visits, and hands-on projects, we help students build the skills and the network to do that work well, and we have a good time doing it.

  • Professional Development

    Resume reviews, co-op prep, and skill workshops in tools like Python, simulation, and Lean Six Sigma.

  • Industry Connections

    Speaker nights, site tours, and networking with alumni and engineers working in operations, consulting, healthcare, and tech.

  • Community

    A place to meet other IE and systems students: study sessions, socials, and a semester project you build as a team.
